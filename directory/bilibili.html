Bilibili

弹幕视频网 — a video community where watching is only half the experience.

Why it's here

Bilibili began as an ACG-focused upload site and grew into one of the largest video platforms in China. What sets it apart is the culture around it: danmaku overlays, long-form creator essays, study livestreams, and niche communities that rarely surface on generic algorithm feeds.

It is listed here not only for entertainment, but as a living archive of internet subcultures, fan translations, technical tutorials, and everyday creativity — often annotated in real time by the audience itself.
